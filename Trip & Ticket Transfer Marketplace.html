Safe Escrow Architecture

How TripRelay Protects Buyers & Sellers

No scams, no zero-refund heartbreaks. Here is our 4-step secure transfer protocol.

1

Post Booking

Seller posts their non-refundable booking along with verification proof & discount.

2

Escrow Payment

Buyer claims the deal. Funds are locked securely in TripRelay Escrow vault.

3

Name Transfer

Seller updates guest name / passes authorization email to buyer.

4

Funds Released

Once buyer confirms booking validity at check-in/travel, payment is released to seller.
